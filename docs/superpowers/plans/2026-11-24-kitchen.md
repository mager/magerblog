# Kitchen Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Rename the public Cooking section to Kitchen and make its growing recipe library easier to browse.

**Architecture:** Keep Astro's static collection and the shared progressive list/search controller. Move the archive to `/kitchen/`, retain legacy redirects, and add exact authored-tag filtering alongside recipe categories and full-text search. Preserve recipe permalinks and reading layouts.

**Tech stack:** Astro, TypeScript, publication CSS tokens, Node tests, JSDOM.

## Design

Use a compact editorial recipe index inspired by Bon Appétit's photographic browsing and Smitten Kitchen's counted taxonomy. A strong Kitchen masthead leads into search, category filters, and a tag selector with popular shortcuts. Place the newest matching recipe in a two-column feature within a four-column grid, followed by compact photo cards. Tablet and phone layouts use two columns; very narrow screens use one. Photo-less recipes get an intentional typographic treatment.

Maintain the publication's light default, saved dark theme, Space Grotesk typography, copper accents, global header/footer, kitchen light control, and reduced-motion support. Category palettes and introductions remain, but oversized category openings become part of the same compact grid.

## Scope decisions
