# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Magerblog is for builders, friends, curious readers, and future collaborators who want useful notes from someone actively making software, cooking, learning, and living in public. The tech section is primarily for founder-engineers, agent builders, and technical readers who skim quickly but reward real specificity.

## Product Purpose

The site is a personal publication. It should make recent work easy to discover, make older writing feel worth revisiting, and give each category its own mood without breaking the mager.co identity. Success looks like a reader trusting the taste, clicking deeper, and remembering that the site felt authored.

## Brand Personality

Warm, technical, opinionated. The writing should feel calm and precise, closer to a smart build log or personal newspaper than a launch thread. The interface can be sharp and future-facing, but it should not posture.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark SaaS dashboards, fake terminal theater, startup hype pages, empty AI futurism, oversized metric blocks, and repetitive card grids. Avoid copy that sounds designed to be quoted on social media.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the homepage's editorial confidence while giving each category a distinct world.
2. Prefer dense, scannable reading surfaces over decorative whitespace.
3. Let technical credibility come from hierarchy, metadata, and concrete content.
4. Use accent color as information, not confetti.
5. Make mobile feel like the primary reading experience, not a compressed desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for readable contrast, clear focus states, generous tap targets, reduced motion support, and layouts that remain useful on small screens.
