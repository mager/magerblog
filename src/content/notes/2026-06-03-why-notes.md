Adding a notes feed to the blog. The model is Simon Willison's site: the full
thing is readable on the index, no click-through to read two sentences, and
every note still gets a permalink and its own RSS.

The bar for a blog post is high enough that most small things never get written
down. A notes feed lowers it — a link plus a sentence of why it mattered is
worth keeping.
