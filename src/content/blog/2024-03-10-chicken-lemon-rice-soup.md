<div class="recipe-intro">

I make this soup with shredded chicken, jasmine rice, coconut cream, and plenty of lemon. Rice is the main version; for a pasta variation, use acini di pepe and add it near the end of cooking. The instructions for that version are below.

This recipe is partially inspired by the NY Times [Chicken and Rice Soup](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020898-chicken-and-rice-soup) by Ali Slagle, but I've added my own twist with coconut cream and generous amounts of lemon for a richer, more vibrant flavor profile.

</div>

<div class="recipe-body">
<div class="recipe-ingredients">

#### Ingredients

- 1 medium sweet onion
- 2-3 carrots
- 5-6 cloves of garlic
- ½ cup fresh parsley, or a handful
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 64 oz. low sodium chicken broth
- ¾ cup jasmine rice, rinsed until clear water flows through
- 1-2 lemons, yielding ½ cup lemon juice
- 1 can coconut cream (or coconut milk)
- Olive oil, butter, garlic powder, salt, and pepper
- Optional spices: dried oregano, thyme, or bay leaves for extra depth

#### Mexican Variation

- Lime instead of lemon
- Assorted mini sweet peppers instead of carrots
- Add sweet corn and green chilies

</div>

<div class="recipe-instructions">

Rinse and pick ½ cup fresh parsley, then let it dry for chopping later. Dice 1 medium sweet onion into small pieces and cut 2-3 carrots into bite-sized chunks (about ½-inch pieces work well). Grate 5-6 cloves of garlic and set aside.

![Prep ingredients](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczNVKlxbF1CpYz086apoArAfHHunB5NCQF-cAmM4YI2mmGpInIOthmFLFRcJ7LvmcfYvXToKsT1dZuPtecziPZ0JRN6oGyHxyu5acUKd6_lAtH4Lu5-A8I3gWkRlst4ngkcsWxAkP9vAaox-7vzpPqi-=w2696-h1714-s-no-gm)

Heat a large dutch oven over medium-high heat and add a generous pat of butter, a glug of olive oil, and the 1 chopped sweet onion. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder, then cook the onion for 5-7 minutes until softened and lightly golden.

Once the onion has softened, add the 5-6 grated garlic cloves and 2-3 chopped carrots and sauté for 2-3 more minutes. Then add ¾ cup rinsed jasmine rice and continue cooking for 2-3 more minutes.

![Sautéing vegetables](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczP2cn0R0-fqza1bPZi6ZeaC4rviNv5Z-9d6FmQ2C2MnCB3wf35xU5ZA1OCWVkemmDyiIhS3vfcU7eD0Wzz8W6HsMBfj411kFha0Vyh-pEWBzRvE-zOWbaKpxCq0tUtWr5xNuFAwrqeH_bP9cGNfOefm=w2048-h1411-s-no-gm)

Add 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts and 64 oz. (8 cups) low sodium chicken broth. Season with salt and pepper; start with 1 teaspoon of salt and adjust after the chicken is cooked. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for about 20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the rice is tender.

While the soup comes to a boil, squeeze 1-2 lemons to get ½ cup lemon juice and chop the ½ cup parsley. During the simmer time, the rice will absorb the broth and thicken the soup while the chicken cooks through.

Remove the 2 cooked chicken breasts from the pot and shred them with two forks. Return the shredded chicken to the pot along with 1 can coconut cream (or coconut milk), stirring to combine.

![Adding coconut cream](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczPW8UHeaElPMgKRPAIjfobf8wm82q2dKX7Iw6KgTOEr-3Wb8HdXJLtJl77wcY3W6zeci7NSBOI8fyWEh1fEREKXMqmImE-JLVWzLr4sMQf3u64849RKkpsljbA-axK7ReQp1YFml1Uy8J8DB5H5-Pc-=w1024-h768-s-no-gm)

Stir in the ½ cup chopped parsley and ½ cup lemon juice. Taste and adjust the seasoning with additional salt, pepper, or lemon juice as needed. You can let it simmer on low for a few more minutes to meld the flavors, or serve immediately for a brighter, fresher taste.

Serve with crusty French bread or tortilla chips. The rice will absorb more broth as it sits; add a splash of broth or water when reheating to loosen the soup.

#### Acini di Pepe Variation

Replace the ¾ cup jasmine rice with ¾ cup dry acini di pepe. Keep all other ingredient amounts the same.

Skip the rice-toasting step. After cooking the garlic and carrots, add the 2 chicken breasts and 64 oz. (8 cups) broth, then simmer as directed above until the chicken is cooked through. Remove the chicken and shred it.

Bring the broth back to a gentle boil and stir in ¾ cup dry acini di pepe. Cook uncovered for the time on the package, stirring often so the pasta does not stick, and taste for doneness. Add a splash of broth or water if the soup becomes too thick.

Once the pasta is tender, return the shredded chicken and stir in 1 can coconut cream (or coconut milk). Heat gently, then finish with ½ cup lemon juice and ½ cup chopped parsley. Taste and adjust the seasoning before serving. The pasta also absorbs broth as it sits, so add broth or water when reheating leftovers.
