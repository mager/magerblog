<div class="recipe-intro">

I picked up a sugarcone cabbage at the grocery store. I cut it in half, rubbed it with Graza olive oil and garlic powder, and roasted it. Leave the core in so the halves hold together when you flip them.

## Ingredients

- 1 small sugarcone cabbage
- 2 tablespoons Graza olive oil
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder

</div>

<div class="recipe-instructions">

## Method

1. **Heat the oven.** Set it to **400°F convection (205°C)**. Line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment rated for 400°F or higher. Trim it to fit.
2. **Halve the cabbage.** Peel off any damaged leaves from **1 small sugarcone cabbage**, then rinse and dry it. Cut it lengthwise through the stem so both halves keep a piece of the core.
3. **Season both halves.** Rub them all over with **2 tablespoons Graza olive oil total**, then season with **½ teaspoon salt, ¼ teaspoon black pepper, and ½ teaspoon garlic powder total**.
4. **Roast cut side down.** Give the halves some room on the pan. Roast for **25 minutes**.
5. **Flip and finish.** Roast cut side up for **10–20 minutes more**. Check after 10 minutes: a knife should slide easily into the thick base. If it’s still firm, check every 5 minutes. If the leaves are getting too dark, tent loosely with foil.
6. **Serve.** Give each person a half, or cut it into wedges. Trim off any tough core at the table.
