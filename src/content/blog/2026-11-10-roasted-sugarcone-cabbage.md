<div class="recipe-intro">

I found a sugarcone cabbage at the grocery store and wanted to keep the preparation simple: cut it in half lengthwise, add olive oil and seasoning, and roast it. I used Graza olive oil and lined the pan with parchment.

![A whole pale green sugarcone cabbage held upright in two hands, showing its pointed top.](https://sdld3v8bpzf3snqo.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/2026-11-10-roasted-sugarcone-cabbage/whole-cabbage.jpg)

This method uses a 400°F convection oven. Keep the core attached so the halves hold together, and turn them cut side up after 25 minutes. Start checking for tenderness at 35 minutes; the thick base matters more than the color of the outer leaves.

</div>

<div class="recipe-body">
<div class="recipe-ingredients">

## Ingredients

- 1 small sugarcone cabbage
- 2 tablespoons Graza olive oil
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder

</div>

<div class="recipe-instructions">

## Method

1. **Heat the oven.** Set it to **400°F convection (205°C)**. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment rated for at least 400°F. Trim the paper so it stays inside the pan.

2. **Prepare the cabbage.** Remove any damaged outer leaves from **1 small sugarcone cabbage**. Rinse and pat dry. Cut lengthwise from the pointed top through the stem, leaving part of the core attached to each half.

3. **Oil and season.** Rub **2 tablespoons Graza olive oil total** over both halves, including the cut faces and outer leaves. Season both halves with **½ teaspoon salt, ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper, and ½ teaspoon garlic powder total**.

4. **Roast cut side down.** Place the halves cut side down on the parchment, with space between them. Roast for **25 minutes**.

5. **Turn and finish.** Turn both halves cut side up. Roast for **10–20 minutes more**, checking after the first 10 minutes. The cabbage is ready when a knife slides easily into the thick base near the core. If it is still firm, keep roasting and check every 5 minutes. If the leaves get too dark before the base softens, cover loosely with foil.

6. **Serve.** Serve a half per person, or cut each half into smaller wedges. Trim away any tough core on the plate.

## Timing

Allow about **35–45 minutes in the oven**, plus 5 minutes for preparation. A larger cabbage can take longer. The flip happens at **25 minutes total**; there is no need to turn it before then.
