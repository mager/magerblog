<div class="recipe-intro">

This recipe is inspired by Brittany Mullins' [Detox Soup](https://www.eatingbirdfood.com/detox-soup). A nourishing, veggie-packed soup with turmeric and ginger that will reset your system.

</div>

<div class="recipe-body">
<div class="recipe-ingredients">

#### Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive or avocado oil
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 2 large carrots, chopped into ¼-inch rounds
- 8 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
- Large handful of mini bell peppers, julienned
- 6 oz chopped shitake mushrooms
- 1 head of broccoli, chopped into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tablespoon freshly grated ginger
- 2 tablespoons freshly grated turmeric
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne
- 2 teaspoons sea salt
- ½ teaspoon ground pepper
- 1 lb chicken breast
- 6 cups chicken stock
- 1 13.5 oz can coconut cream
- Juice from half of a lemon
- 1 tablespoon tamari, coconut aminos or soy sauce
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, plus more for topping

</div>

<div class="recipe-instructions">

In a large Dutch oven or soup pot, add oil and butter. Once hot, add onion and sauté for 10 minutes. Next, add the carrots, broccoli and peppers and sauté for 5-6 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add mushrooms, garlic, ginger, turmeric, cayenne, salt and pepper. Sauté for a few minutes longer.

![Sautéing vegetables](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczP5KyJ9wxXFlcQlRI0xZLJpoWnOlRMfCFkYcbiOq8bAxpnireTnbmBcH_0g4faNEbozt1TXMl_U_0ggkSuISc1N_jCQEIjJGSjw_D_IiYQvFDBn53TAYmCpa5ABxX3LGRAnsJ5GoiCfNo4uEwWgXtgc=w2696-h1714-s-no-gm)

Add chicken broth and chicken to the pot. Bring the mixture to a boil, reduce heat to a simmer and cover. Simmer for about 20 minutes or until chicken is fully cooked.

![Simmering soup](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczNO1O3y5BPx31Odm9Y6O9rXTA_YyD38etlgi-vElHS-qfgeT__90uDfRiSTCNrv5kq7zXABILWFmFDm5rFgTQ1X5petgMct-j51O7UhGo-m0W-4spjikmHrto8SKZFl-Dik5f5NBHBIedShgM2I8vTt=w2048-h1536-s-no-gm)

When chicken is fully cooked, remove from pot and chop the chicken. Place chicken back into the soup and stir in fresh cilantro, tamari, coconut cream and lemon juice.

![Adding chicken back](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczMZcs8XrSNABuDm4XFBSA1xRHvEfmfA7vwkrOFquFw0fOh0efX_UBe-TnJ62smn74zp_9oqLLeF6Ca_G_aONKKOzXPSaLgnp3H9n8wOIlf0a6uVnt087qcMxXorm6UUIX4wFBMU0hR6KMvyGV5w3CgC=w1286-h1714-s-no-gm)

Serve with extra chopped cilantro on top and side of choice. Tear a piece of french baguette off if it's a cheat day.
