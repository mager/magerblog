I'm adapting [Makinze Gore's turkey tetrazzini recipe at Delish](https://www.delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/a25239697/easy-turkey-tetrazzini-recipe/) with linguine and a Parmesan topping. Leftover turkey, mushrooms, and peas go into a white wine cream sauce before baking.

Use a deep 9-by-13-inch baking dish. Allow about an hour for prep and cooking, plus a **10-minute rest** before serving.

## Ingredients

### Pasta and sauce

- Cooking spray, enough to coat the baking dish
- Kosher salt, for the pasta water and to taste in the sauce
- 1 lb dry linguine
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided: 2 tablespoons for the mushrooms and 4 tablespoons for the sauce
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 lb cremini mushrooms, cleaned and sliced
- ½ cup dry white wine
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 2 lb cooked turkey, chopped into bite-size pieces (about 5 cups)
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 cup shredded white cheddar
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano

### Topping

- ½ cup finely grated Parmesan

## Method

1. **Prepare the dish and pasta.** Heat the oven to **350°F**. Coat a deep **9-by-13-inch baking dish** with **cooking spray**. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add **kosher salt**. Cook **1 lb linguine** to al dente using the package timing, then drain.
2. **Cook the mushrooms.** Melt **2 tablespoons unsalted butter** in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Stir in **2 finely chopped garlic cloves** for about **1 minute**. Add **1 lb sliced cremini mushrooms** and **½ cup dry white wine**. Cook, stirring, for about **5 minutes**, until the mushrooms soften and very little wine remains in the pot.
3. **Build the sauce.** Melt the remaining **4 tablespoons unsalted butter** into the mushrooms. Stir in **¼ cup all-purpose flour** and cook for **3 minutes**, stirring so it does not catch on the bottom. Gradually stir in **2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth**, followed by **1 cup heavy cream**, smoothing out any flour clumps. Simmer for about **5 minutes**, stirring often, until the sauce coats a spoon. Season with **kosher salt and black pepper to taste**.
4. **Combine the filling.** Fold in **2 lb chopped cooked turkey (about 5 cups)**, **1 cup frozen peas**, **1 cup shredded white cheddar**, and **1 teaspoon dried oregano**. Add the cooked **1 lb linguine** and toss until the sauce reaches all the noodles. Taste and adjust **salt and pepper**, then spread the mixture in the prepared dish.
5. **Add the topping.** Sprinkle **½ cup finely grated Parmesan** evenly over the pasta.
6. **Bake and rest.** Bake uncovered for about **25 minutes**, until the Parmesan is lightly browned, the sauce bubbles at the edges, and the center reaches **165°F**. If the topping browns before the center is hot, cover loosely with foil and continue baking. Rest for **10 minutes** before serving.
