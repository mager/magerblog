I've been making this on rotation since I found [Bon Appétit's Make-Ahead Lentil Salad](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/make-ahead-lentil-salad). It's one of those recipes that photographs well, travels well to a friend's place, and actually improves after sitting in the fridge overnight. That trifecta is rare.

The genius move is the infused oil. You don't make a vinaigrette — you build flavor directly in a skillet with olive oil, garlic, lemon peel, cumin seeds, scallion whites, and red pepper flakes, then pour that fragrant oil over warm lentils while they can still absorb it. The lentils soak up everything.

Castelvetrano olives and feta finish it. If you've never had Castelvetrano olives, they're the big buttery green ones — less briny than a standard olive, almost creamy. They make this dish.

Credit goes entirely to BA. I'm just here to evangelize it.

## Ingredients

Serves 4.

- 1½ cups black beluga or French green lentils
- 1 large bunch Tuscan kale, stems removed, leaves torn or chopped
- 3 scallions, white and green parts separated, thinly sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
- Strips of peel from 1 lemon (use a peeler — you want wide strips, not zest)
- ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp cumin seeds
- ½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- ½ cup raw almonds
- 5 oz feta, crumbled
- 1 cup Castelvetrano olives, pitted and roughly torn
- Kosher salt

## Method

**1. Cook the lentils.**
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the lentils and simmer until tender but still holding their shape — about 20–25 minutes. You want al dente, not mushy. Drain and transfer to a large bowl.

**2. Build the infused oil.**
While the lentils cook, combine the olive oil, garlic cloves, lemon peel strips, scallion whites, cumin seeds, and red pepper flakes in a skillet. Set over medium heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until the garlic is golden and everything smells deeply fragrant — about 5–7 minutes. Don't rush this; low and slow extracts more flavor.

**3. Dress while warm.**
Pour the infused oil (garlic, lemon peel, and all) over the warm lentils. Toss to coat thoroughly. The warmth helps the lentils absorb the oil rather than just wearing it.

**4. Fold in the rest.**
Add the kale, almonds, olives, and scallion greens. Toss again. The residual heat from the lentils will wilt the kale slightly — just enough to soften it without cooking it down.

**5. Top with feta and serve.**
Crumble the feta over the top. Serve warm, at room temperature, or cold. As promised in the title: it's better the next day.

## Notes

- Black beluga lentils hold their shape the best and look striking. French green (Puy) lentils are a close second. Avoid red or yellow lentils — they'll turn to mush.
- Fish out the lemon peel strips before serving if you want, but I leave them in. They're soft by that point and anyone who gets one is getting a good bite.
- The almonds add crunch. Toast them first if you have five minutes — it's worth it.
- This keeps well in the fridge for 4–5 days. Add a splash of olive oil when you pull it out if it looks dry.
