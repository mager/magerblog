<p>A whole chicken, a Dutch oven, and one uninterrupted hour in the oven. The rice cooks in turmeric- and saffron-stained broth right underneath the bird, soaking up everything. We add frozen veggies to the mix for extra body. Adapted from <a href="https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024109-one-pot-whole-roasted-chicken-and-rice">Naz Deravian's One-Pot Whole Roasted Chicken and Rice</a> on NYT Cooking.</p>

<div class="recipe-body">
<div class="recipe-ingredients">

#### Saffron Water (optional but worth it)

- Small pinch saffron threads
- Pinch granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons hot water

#### Chicken & Rice

- 1 whole chicken (4–5 lbs), trimmed of excess fat
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped
- 2 large garlic cloves, chopped
- 1½ teaspoons ground turmeric, divided
- 1½ cups white basmati rice, rinsed and drained
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2½ cups water
- 1–2 cups frozen vegetables (peas, carrots, or a mix — add with the rice)
- Fresh parsley or cilantro, for garnish

</div>

<div class="recipe-instructions">

Shoutout to [Naz Deravian](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024109-one-pot-whole-roasted-chicken-and-rice) for the original — this one's a true showstopper:

Heat the oven to 375°F. If using saffron, grind it with the sugar in a mortar and pestle to a fine powder, add the hot (not boiling) water, stir, cover, and let steep.

Pat the chicken dry and season generously all over — inside the cavity too — with 5–7 teaspoons of kosher salt (scale to your bird's weight) and about 2 teaspoons of black pepper. This can be done up to 24 hours ahead; just leave it uncovered in the fridge and pull it out 30 minutes before cooking.

In a large (6-quart) Dutch oven, heat 3 tablespoons of olive oil over medium. Add the onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden, 7–9 minutes. Reduce to medium-low, season with a little salt, add the garlic, and cook 1–2 minutes until soft. Add ½ teaspoon turmeric and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Turn off the heat and transfer the onion mixture to a small bowl. Don't wash the pot.

Rub the remaining 1 teaspoon of turmeric all over the chicken. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil in the same pot over medium-high. Place the chicken breast-side down and cook until golden, about 5 minutes — you're not going for full crispy skin, just color. Flip carefully with tongs and a wooden spoon and brown the back side, another 5 minutes.

Scatter the onion mixture around (not over) the chicken, then drizzle 2½ cups of water in from the sides of the pot — never over the bird. Bring to a quick boil, cover, and place in the oven for 50 minutes.

Carefully pull the pot out. Spoon the rice evenly into the liquid around the chicken, nudging any stray grains off the bird and into the broth. Scatter in the frozen vegetables. Drizzle the lemon juice and saffron water over the chicken, cover, and return to the oven for 30 minutes. Let it rest, covered, for 5 minutes before serving.

Carve right in the pot or lift onto a board. Either way, serve with the rice scooped alongside and a handful of fresh herbs on top.
