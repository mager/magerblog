</div>

<div class="recipe-body">
<div class="recipe-ingredients">

## Ingredients

- ½ rotisserie chicken, meat pulled from the bones
- 2 heads romaine, chopped into 1-inch strips
- 10 oz dry fusilli
- Shaved Parmesan, to taste
- Cindy's Kitchen Roasted Garlic Caesar dressing, enough to coat
- Juice of ½ lemon, optional

</div>

<div class="recipe-instructions">

## Method

1. **Cook the pasta.** Cook **10 oz fusilli** according to the package directions. Drain, rinse under cold water until cool, and drain well.
2. **Prep the chicken and lettuce.** Pull the meat from **½ rotisserie chicken** into bite-size pieces. Wash and dry **2 heads romaine**, then chop into **1-inch strips**.
3. **Toss.** Combine the cooled fusilli, pulled chicken, and romaine in a large bowl. Add **shaved Parmesan to taste** and enough **Cindy's Kitchen Roasted Garlic Caesar dressing** to lightly coat everything. Toss, then add more dressing if needed.
4. **Finish and serve.** Squeeze in the **juice of ½ lemon**, if using, and toss again. Serve right away so the romaine stays crisp.
