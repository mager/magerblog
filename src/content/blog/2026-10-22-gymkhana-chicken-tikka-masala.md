I browned the chicken thighs with the yogurt marinade still on them, then moved them to a plate while I cooked the vegetables. The onions cooked for 6 minutes, followed by 4 more minutes with the red pepper. I then combined everything with the chickpeas and Gymkhana Tikka Masala sauce for a final 5-minute simmer and served it with basmati rice.

## Method

1. **Prepare the rice.** Cook the basmati rice according to its package directions. Keep it warm for serving.
2. **Marinate the chicken.** Coat the chicken thighs in the yogurt marinade. The yogurt amount, any other marinade ingredients, and the marinating time still need to be recorded.
3. **Brown the chicken first.** Sear the chicken thighs with the yogurt marinade still on them until browned. Transfer the chicken to a plate. The heat setting and searing time were not recorded.
4. **Cook the onions for 6 minutes.** Add the onions to the same pan and sauté for 6 minutes, stirring as needed.
5. **Add the red pepper for 4 more minutes.** Add the red pepper to the onions. Sauté together for another 4 minutes.
6. **Combine and simmer.** Return the chicken and its juices to the pan. Add the chickpeas and Gymkhana Tikka Masala simmer sauce. Stir to combine and simmer for 5 minutes, as in this batch. Check the thickest part of the chicken with a food thermometer; it must reach 165°F (74°C). Continue cooking if needed. Browning and a 5-minute simmer alone do not establish that the chicken is cooked through.
7. **Serve.** Spoon the chicken, vegetables, chickpeas, and sauce over the cooked basmati rice.
