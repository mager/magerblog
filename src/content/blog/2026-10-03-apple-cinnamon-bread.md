I built this recipe around apples from [Apple Holler](https://www.appleholler.com/) in Wisconsin: buttery sautéed apples, yogurt batter, and a crisp cinnamon-sugar top. These photos are from my first bake.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour
- 1½ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp fine salt
- 3 tsp ground cinnamon, divided
- 8 tbsp (113 g) unsalted butter, divided
- ½ cup (100 g) packed light brown sugar, divided
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- ½ cup (120 g) plain whole-milk yogurt, at room temperature
- ¼ cup (60 ml) milk
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 3 cups (360 g) peeled, cored, diced apples (about 3 medium or 4 small; measured raw)
- 1 tbsp (12 g) granulated sugar, for the top

## Method

1. **Mix the dry ingredients.** Whisk **2 cups (240 g) flour**, **1½ tsp baking powder**, **½ tsp baking soda**, **½ tsp salt**, and **1½ tsp cinnamon** in a large bowl. Set aside.

2. **Prep the oven and pan.** Heat the oven to **350°F conventional or 325°F convection**, with a rack in the center. Butter a **9 × 5-inch metal loaf pan** and line with parchment. Mix **½ tsp cinnamon** with the **1 tbsp granulated sugar** for the topping.

3. **Cook the apples.** Melt **2 tbsp butter** in a wide skillet over medium heat. Add **3 cups (360 g) peeled, cored apples**, cut into **⅜-inch dice**, and cook **8–12 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until tender with golden edges and no pooled juice. Stir in **1 tbsp packed brown sugar** and cook **1–2 minutes**. Add **1 tsp cinnamon** and toss briefly to coat all the apples, **no more than 30 seconds**. Take off the heat as soon as they’re coated; cooking longer can make the sugar-and-cinnamon coating sticky and hard as it cools. Spread the apples and buttery coating on a plate to cool for about **10 minutes**, until barely warm.

   ![Diced peeled apples sautéing in a skillet, with golden edges beginning to form.](https://sdld3v8bpzf3snqo.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/2026-10-03-apple-cinnamon-bread/sauteed-apples.jpg)

4. **Mix the wet ingredients.** Melt the **remaining 6 tbsp butter** and let it cool until warm, not hot. Whisk with the **remaining 7 tbsp packed brown sugar (88 g)**, then **2 large eggs**, **½ cup (120 g) yogurt**, **¼ cup (60 ml) milk**, and **2 tsp vanilla** until smooth.

5. **Combine.** Fold the wet ingredients into the dry until a few flour streaks remain. Add **all the cooled apples and their buttery coating**, then gently fold until **no dry flour is visible**. Stop mixing there; the batter will be thick.

6. **Bake.** Spoon into the pan, smooth the top, and sprinkle with all the prepared cinnamon sugar (**1 tbsp sugar + ½ tsp cinnamon**). Bake **55–65 minutes**, checking at 50. Tent loosely with foil if the top browns early. A skewer in the center should come out without wet batter; try another spot if you hit an apple.

7. **Cool.** Leave in the pan for **15 minutes**, then lift onto a rack. Wait at least **1 hour** before slicing so the center can set.

## A few notes

- **Apples:** Use firm apples, ideally a mix of sweet and tart. Measure the **3 cups (360 g)** after peeling and coring, before cooking. Use the whole cooked batch.
- **Flour:** Weigh it, or spoon into the measuring cup and level off.
- **Timing:** Allow about **1¾ hours through baking**, plus **75 minutes** to cool. The listed cook time includes the skillet and oven.

## Storage

Cool completely before wrapping. Keep covered at cool room temperature for up to **2 days**, or refrigerate for up to **5 days**. Freeze individual slices for longer storage and rewarm in a toaster oven.
